NIHAL VIJAYKUMAR DESAVALE ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Namaste Jyotish mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed while using our App.

No User Sign-In or Account Required: General users are NOT required to create an account, register, or provide an email, phone number, or login credentials to use Namaste Jyotish. All core features (Kundali generation, Dasha calculations, PDF export, and Astrologer chat) are fully accessible without user registration.

1. Information Processing & Architecture

Namaste Jyotish is built on a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture. Most astrological calculations—such as Kundali generation (D1 to D60 charts), Bhava Chalit, planetary positions, Vimshottari Dasha calculations, Ashtakavarga, Gochar (transits), Matchmaking, Vastu direction analysis, PDF generation, and Panchang—are computed 100% locally on your device.

A. Birth Details and Kundali Profiles

  • Data Handled: Name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth.
  • Storage Location: Stored strictly in your device's private on-device local database (Room/SQLite).
  • Access: We do not have access to your saved local Kundalis or private profiles. This data remains on your physical device.

B. Astrologer Directory & Live Consultation Chat

  • Direct Chat for Users: Users can initiate consultation chat with astrologers directly without creating an account or signing in.
  • Cloud Transmission (Firebase Firestore): The consultation messages and the astrological summary you choose to share are transmitted securely via Google Cloud Firebase Firestore so the astrologer can read and respond.
  • Astrologer Portal: Only authorized astrologers log in using their assigned portal credentials to answer queries.

C. PDF Export & Document Generation

The App allows you to generate and export comprehensive Vedic Kundali reports as high-resolution PDF documents. These documents are compiled locally on your device and can be shared or saved via standard Android system share intents under your direct control.

D. Palmistry (Hastrekha) Analysis & Media Handling

  • Zero Camera Permission: The App does NOT request or use android.permission.CAMERA.
  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: The App does NOT request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.
  • Privacy-Preserving Photo Picker: When analyzing palm lines, the App uses Android's modern Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). The App only receives temporary access to the specific photo you manually select.

E. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features

The App features Vedic astrological analysis powered by Google Gemini AI / Firebase AI Logic. When you ask a question in the "Namaste Jyotish" AI tab, only the specific astrological planetary context and your question are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to generate the reading. Your data is not used to train foundational AI models without consent.

2. Device Permissions Disclosed

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch AI astrological interpretations, sync live astrologer consultations, and verify network connectivity.
  • Push Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional on Android 13+. Used only if you choose to receive notifications when an astrologer replies to your chat.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide brief haptic feedback during interactive tools, such as the Vastu compass.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Used to process optional in-app purchases or consultation tokens via Google Play's official billing system.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

All financial transactions are handled exclusively through the Google Play In-App Billing system. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial payment credentials.

4. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete any saved Kundali profile at any time directly within the App via the "Saved Kundalis" screen, or by clearing the App's data in Android Settings.
  • Chat Data Deletion: You can clear your chat history inside the chat screen. If you wish to request deletion of any cloud chat session, you may email us at nickdesavale@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Cloud & Firebase (Firestore): Secure cloud messaging database. (Firebase Privacy Policy)
  • Google Gemini AI: Natural language astrological interpretation engine. (Google Privacy Policy)
