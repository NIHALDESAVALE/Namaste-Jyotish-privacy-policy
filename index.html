NIHAL VIJAYKUMAR DESAVALE ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Namaste Jyotish mobile application (the "App"), with the package name com.aistudio.jyotishai.qzkajh. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We build our App to be privacy-first. Namaste Jyotish performs most of its calculations, including Kundali generation and planetary tracking, locally on your device (offline). We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, birth details, or generated charts to our servers.

Birth Details and Profiles

Any data you enter, such as your name, date of birth, time of birth, and location (latitude/longitude), is saved locally on your device's internal storage using a local database. We do not have access to this information.

API Key Integration (AI Features)

To use the AI-powered features (such as AI Kundali Analysis, Matchmaking Reports, etc.), the App requires you to provide your own third-party API Key (e.g., Google Gemini API Key).

  • Your API Key is stored securely and locally on your device using encrypted data storage preferences.
  • When you request an AI analysis, the App sends your locally stored birth details and astrological charts directly to the third-party AI provider (e.g., Google) using your API key.
  • We do not intercept, collect, or store your API Key or the data sent to and received from the AI provider.
  • Please review the Privacy Policy of the API provider (e.g., Google) to understand how they handle the data transmitted via their API.

2. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. Since all personal information and API keys are stored locally on your device, we rely on Android's built-in security mechanisms to protect your data. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage on a mobile device is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security if your device is compromised.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising libraries that collect your personal information. The only external communication happens when you actively use the AI features, which communicate directly with the AI provider using the API key you supply.

4. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Internet Access: Required ONLY when communicating with the third-party AI API (using your provided API key) to fetch astrological insights.

We do not use permissions to access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone unless explicitly required for a specific feature you initiate.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13 ("Children"). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. Since all data is stored locally, parents or guardians have full control over the information entered into the App on their devices.
