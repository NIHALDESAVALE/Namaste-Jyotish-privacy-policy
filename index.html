NIHAL VIJAYKUMAR DESAVALE ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Namaste Jyotish mobile application (the "App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you enter while using our App.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, how it is stored and protected, how our AI and cloud consultation features operate, and your data protection rights.

1. Information Processing & Core Architecture

Namaste Jyotish is designed with a privacy-first, offline-capable architecture. Most astrological calculations—such as Kundali generation (D1 to D60 charts), Bhava Chalit, planetary positions, Vimshottari Dasha calculations, Ashtakavarga, Gochar (transits), Matchmaking (Guna Milan), Vastu direction analysis, and Panchang—are computed 100% locally on your device without transmitting data to external servers.

A. Birth Details and Kundali Profiles

  • Data Handled: Full name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth (city, latitude, longitude, and timezone).
  • Storage Location: Stored strictly in your device's private on-device local database (Room/SQLite).
  • Access: We do not have access to your saved local Kundalis or private profiles. This data remains on your physical device unless you explicitly share a Kundali summary during an Astrologer consultation chat.

B. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features & Gemini API

The App features Vedic astrological analysis and chat interpretation powered by Google Gemini AI / Firebase AI Logic.

  • What is Sent: When you submit a question in the "Namaste Jyotish" AI tab or request an AI Vedic synthesis, only the specific astrological planetary context and your query are sent securely via encrypted HTTPS to Google AI services for generating the interpretation.
  • No Model Training: Your private astrological questions and charts are processed solely to provide real-time responses and are not used to train foundational AI models without your authorization.
  • Data Transmission: All AI requests are transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption.

C. Palmistry (Hastrekha) Analysis & Media Handling

  • Zero Camera Permission: The App does NOT request or use android.permission.CAMERA.
  • Zero Broad Storage Permissions: The App does NOT request READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.
  • Secure Photo Picker: When you choose to analyze palm lines, the App uses Android's privacy-preserving system Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia). The App only receives temporary access to the specific image you manually select.
  • Image Usage: Selected palm images are used solely to analyze hand lines and are never used for facial recognition, biometric identity tracking, or advertising.

D. Astrologer Directory & Cloud Consultation Chat

When you choose to consult with verified astrologers inside the App:

  • Authentication: Users and astrologers authenticate securely via Google Sign-In using the Android Credential Manager API. We receive standard Google profile identifiers (such as your display name, email address, and profile photo) strictly for account verification.
  • Cloud Storage (Firebase Firestore): Chat messages, consultation history, and any Kundali summary you deliberately attach to a chat are stored securely in Google Cloud Firebase Firestore.
  • Access Control: Secure Firestore security rules enforce that only the specific user and the astrologer involved in the consultation session can read or write messages within that consultation channel.

2. Device Permissions Disclosed

The App requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to fetch AI astrological interpretations, sync cloud astrologer consultations, and verify network connectivity.
  • Push Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional on Android 13+. Used only if you enable notifications for astrologer consultation replies or daily Panchang updates. You can revoke this permission anytime in your system settings.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide brief haptic feedback during interactive tools, such as the Vastu compass and wheel alignments.
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING): Used to process optional in-app purchases or premium consultation tokens via Google Play's official billing system.

Note: We do NOT access your Contacts, Call Logs, SMS, precise GPS location, Camera, or general device storage.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

All financial transactions are handled exclusively through the Google Play In-App Billing system. We do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial payment credentials. All billing data is governed by Google Play's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal information:

  • Local Data: You can delete any saved Kundali profile at any time directly within the App via the "Saved Kundalis" screen, or by clearing the App's data in Android Settings.
  • Cloud Data Deletion: If you have participated in an Astrologer consultation and wish to permanently delete your cloud account, messages, or consultation records, you may email us at nickdesavale@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process and delete your cloud records within 30 days in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
